Model · 모델
• 10 DOF: platform (6) + passive 2-axis gimbal carrying the drone (roll, pitch) + pendulum tail (2). RK4, Δt = 0.5 ms. Display shows simulation time; it may run slower than real time.
• Drone: 249 g, inertia of a 298 × 373 × 101 mm body (DJI Mini 4 Pro class). Its CoM hangs d below the gimbal pivot.
• Legs per side: rigid femur → knee (pitch; spring + one-way damper) → lower leg → ankle U-joint (pitch: spring + one-way damper; roll: spring + two-way damper) → foot → claw on the branch (rotational stiffness on 3 axes).
• Leg segments are massless; their mass and the gimbal yoke are lumped into the platform (90 g).
• Drag on the drone: CdA ≈ 0.012 m² (estimate). Grip capacity = lock + μ·η·N·r.
• All spring, damper, claw and tail values are design assumptions, not measurements. Use this to choose directions; the test rig decides.
